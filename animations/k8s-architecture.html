Click any component to learn what it does. The Control Plane is the brain that makes decisions; the Worker Nodes are the muscle that runs your apps.

🏥 Analogy: A Kubernetes cluster is like a hospital. The Control Plane is the administration & doctors' office (it decides who goes where and keeps records); the Worker Nodes are the hospital wards where patients (your app pods) actually stay. The API server is the reception desk everyone must go through, and etcd is the master records room.
