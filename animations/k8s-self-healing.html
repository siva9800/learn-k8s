☸️ Kubernetes Self-Healing & Auto-Scaling

Kubernetes constantly compares desired state (what you asked for) with actual state (what's running) and fixes any difference. Break things below and watch it heal.

🏪 Analogy: Think of a shop manager told "always keep 3 staff on the floor." If one goes home sick, the manager immediately calls a replacement. If the whole shop floor closes, they reopen it elsewhere with 3 staff again. You never manage individuals — you declare the desired number, and Kubernetes (the manager) makes reality match it. This is the reconciliation loop.
